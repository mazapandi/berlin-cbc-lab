The model

Utility function

Vij = T + D + P + R + C + F + G + U
Pij = SOFTMAX(V / λ),   λ = 1.5,   Vopt-out = 0
T
Theme fit (history, local, art, custom)
D
Duration preference (short vs. long)
P
Price penalty (per €10 of payment)
R
Private format bonus
C
Customization bonus (private only)
F
Fixed-price vs. tips preference
G
Group tolerance (public tours only)
U
Tip uncertainty penalty (tips tours)

What this is & isn't

Synthetic study, controlled ground truth

10 Spanish-speaking traveller archetypes · 12 choice tasks each · 6 real tour products · reproducible with seed 42.

Preferences are invented and known to me. This demonstrates the mechanism and lets me check whether CBC recovers what I programmed. Not a market study.

Preference recovery: 7 of 10 archetypes land on their expected modal choice. R06 (Family Organizer) shows a correctly-identified edge case between private and opt-out.

Head-to-head H2 vs H3.5 (tasks 5–8): H2 wins 10 of 40 decisions, H3.5 wins 4, opt-out and other alternatives take the remaining 26. Operational: H2 is 56% more efficient on people-per-guide-hour under these preferences.

Primary sources

  2. Louviere, J. J., & Woodworth, G. (1983). Design and Analysis of Simulated Consumer Choice or Allocation Experiments. Journal of Marketing Research, 20(4), 350–367. — the paper that invented CBC.
  3. Orme, B. K. (2013). Which Conjoint Method Should I Use? Sawtooth Software Research Paper Series. — practical guidance for choosing CBC vs. ACBC vs. CVA by attribute count, sample size, and interview length.
